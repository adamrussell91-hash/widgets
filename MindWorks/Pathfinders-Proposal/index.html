Specialist Extension Pilot · Years 3 to 6
Pilot Proposal · Term III, 2026

A five-week specialist sprint that turns high-potential thinking into a real pathway.

Pathfinders places Years 3 to 6 students of high potential onto one of four specialist lines: Justice, Innovation, Expression and Reasoning, to tackle a single shared challenge, then present a viable first response to parents in Week 5.

"The shared method comes from Future Problem Solving. Each line supplies a specialist lens. The parent showcase supplies the authentic audience."
→
Student choice, staff guided. Students rank their preferred line. Final placement balances interest, readiness and group balance across streams of six to ten students.
